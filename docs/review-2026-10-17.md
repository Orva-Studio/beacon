## Not doing

Measured on a copy of the user's largest project (304 clips) on 2026-10-18 (`docs/performance.md`):

- **P3** (the shell re-renders on every playback tick): 1.4 ms a tick for the whole tree in React's
  development build, under a millisecond to win in production.
- **P5** (a whole-project save after every edit): 0.6 ms (0.9 worst) to stringify, write and fsync 435 KB.
- **P8, P14** (animations sorted on every read; derived-data misses not remembered): evaluating a frame is
  0.05 ms, a missed lookup 1.4 µs.
- **P10** (missing-media checks): one `existsSync` per asset, sent only when the list changes.
- **P15** (the audio mix opens its sources once per segment): 9.5 of the mix's 9.6 s is the AAC encode,
  and the mix runs beside the video render; first audio at play start is about 50 ms with 27 inputs.
- **P17** (a thumbnail is an ffmpeg each, ~220 ms from a `.trec`): goes with Phase 9's filmstrip
  thumbnails, which decode each stream once into a sprite and give these too.

- **B13** (`measureLoudness` and `thumbnailPng` ignore ffmpeg's exit code): not a bug. An empty thumbnail
