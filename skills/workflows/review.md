# Review

1. `project_describe` for structure; `transcript_get` for what is said.
2. `frames_sheet` across the whole timeline (about one frame per 4 s, 4-6
   columns), then closer sheets around every cut and zoom.
3. Check: `transcript_joins` with `chopped: true` is empty (no audible
   skips); no doubled or missing words at cuts; no black or empty frames
   (gaps on the screen track); zooms frame what the narration talks about;
   the camera (if shown) doesn't cover what matters; no long dead air.
4. Report each problem with its time and a proposed fix (command + args).
   Apply fixes only if asked.
